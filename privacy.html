Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Team Walter Games ("we," "us," or "our") collects and uses information when you use the ThisClose website and game (the "Service").

Information We Collect

We do not ask for or collect personally identifiable information through the Service. You don't need an account to play.

Game data on your device

Your team names, scores and custom card decks are saved in your browser's local storage so your game is still there when you come back. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Anonymous daily game counts

To see how the game is played, your browser adds up a short, fixed list of game events each day, such as how many rounds were started or revealed and how many points each round scored. Once a day is over, it sends just those totals. They include the kind of device (phone, tablet or desktop) and nothing else: no names, no clue text, no card text, no identifiers and no IP addresses are stored.

Usage data

We use Google Analytics to understand how the Service is used, such as which pages are visited and roughly how long people play. Google Analytics collects information like your browser type, device type, approximate location and pages viewed, and uses cookies to do so. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles this data. You can block cookies in your browser settings and still play.

Advertising

The Service does not show advertising.

Children

ThisClose is a family game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game works without any sign-up or personal details.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at teamwaltergames@gmail.com.
