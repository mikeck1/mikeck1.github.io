How to play

  1. 1
    Pick a Clue Giver. Hand them the phone. They tap to see the secret spot on the bar. Everyone else, no peeking!
  2. 2
    Give a clue. The bar runs between two opposites, like Cold and Hot. Say one word or phrase that lands on the spot. Is the spot "pretty Hot"? Try "hot chocolate."
  3. 3
    Hide & pass. Hide the spot and pass the phone to your team. They talk it over and drag the marker to their guess.
  4. 4
    Reveal! Lock it in to see how close you got. Then the next team takes a turn.
