How to play

  1. 1
    Pass to the psychic. One player on the current team is the Psychic. They tap the dial to peek at the hidden target. Everyone else, no looking!
  2. 2
    Give a clue. The card shows two opposites, like Cold ↔ Hot. The Psychic says one word or phrase that sits where the target is. Aiming a bit toward Hot? Try "warm soup."
  3. 3
    Hide & guess. The Psychic closes the dial, and the team talks it over and drags the needle to their guess.
  4. 4
    Reveal! Open the dial to see how close you got. Then the next team takes a turn.
