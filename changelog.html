November 2026: Introducing ThisClose

  • A new game, a new look. Welcome to ThisClose! Two opposites, one secret spot, and one clue to get your team there.
  • The bar. Slide the marker along a bar between two opposites. It tells you where you are in words, like "Sorta Hot" or "Very Cold."
  • Points by distance. Score 10 for spot on, and 1 point less for every 2 steps away.
  • Pass the phone. A full-screen hand-off makes sure only the Clue Giver sees the spot.
  • No ads. The game is ad-free.
  • Your stuff comes with you. Custom decks and team names from before carry over automatically.
  • Deck editor. Write your own cards, search the deck, and import or export decks to share with friends.
  • Works offline. Once loaded, the game keeps working without an internet connection.
